Speech recognition

Readings use a hiragana speech model.

Recognition runs inside your browser, on this device. Your voice is never sent anywhere. It also offers other answers it heard (e.g. "hand" for "and", or にん for じん): press their numbertap one to switch before you submit.

More choices offer the right answer more often (readings: 270 of 400 recordings at 3, 288 at 5), but there's more to look through, and English takes a little longer above 3.

WaniKani dictionary

Among the answers heard, ones that are the meaning or reading of any WaniKani item (any level, not just this question) are offered first. Nothing heard is replaced: a wrong answer still goes in as you said it. The dictionary is a file you make from your own WaniKani API token with tools/wk-dictionary.mjs (see the README); it stays on this computer, and the add-on itself never contacts WaniKani.
