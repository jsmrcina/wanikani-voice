Speech recognition

Recognition runs inside your browser, on this device. Your voice is never sent anywhere. It also offers up to two other answers it heard (e.g. "hand" for "and", or にん for じん): press 1–3 to switch before you submit.

Custom models

Use your own fine-tuned model instead of the built-in one. Model files (.wkv-model.zip) are made with tools/pack-model.mjs and stay on this computer.
